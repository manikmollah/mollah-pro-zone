      What cannot be hidden
      
                
      
                  Honesty matters more than marketing here. A proxy-based VPN changes your
                  IP address, your device timezone and your WebRTC exposure.
                  It cannot change:
                
      
                
        
                  
      • The fact that the exit node is a hosting/datacenter IP — some sites flag those regardless of country
        • 
                  
      • Your DNS resolver — queries still leave through your configured DNS
        • 
                  
      • Whether this specific free server is already on a public blocklist
        • 
                  
      • Login history, cookies or accounts that already know you
        • 
                
      
                
      
                  Tip: to look consistent, also set your browser language, and prefer normal
                  HTTPS browsing over sites that require a phone number.
